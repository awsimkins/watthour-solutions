Seasonal Field Technician Assistant

Join our team supporting precision metering and utility field work across the Midwest.

About the Role

Watthour Solutions is looking for reliable, hardworking individuals to support our seasonal field operations. This is hands-on work assisting with utility metering projects, CT meter testing, audits, and related fieldwork. Most work will be out of town, often requiring overnight travel. You’ll work alongside experienced technicians in a variety of conditions, including heat and outdoor environments.

Requirements

Must Have:

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Valid driver’s license with clean driving record
  • No felonies
  • Able to lift and carry 50 lbs repeatedly
  • Comfortable working in the heat and around electricity

Helpful / Preferred:

  • Basic electricity or metering knowledge
  • Previous utility, construction, or field work experience
  • Willingness to travel and stay out of town for extended periods

What to Expect

Travel

Most work is out of town. You should be comfortable with overnight travel and varying schedules.

Seasonal

These are seasonal positions tied to project needs. Start dates vary throughout the year.
