OUR STORY

Decades of Expertise.
Rebranded for the Future.

Watthour Solutions builds on more than 30 years of hands-on experience in electric metering and utility optimization. Founded by Anthony W. Simkins, we previously operated as Innovative Electric Services and have now rebranded to better reflect our expanded capabilities and commitment to serving Midwest utilities.

We specialize in optimizing utility revenue through precise metering audits, accurate field testing, and strategic billing corrections. Our work has helped electric cooperatives and utilities recover substantial lost revenue while improving metering accuracy and operational efficiency.
