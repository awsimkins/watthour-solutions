OUR STORY

Built on Decades of
Hands-On Experience

Watthour Solutions was founded by Anthony W. Simkins, who brings more than 30 years of real-world experience in electric metering and utility operations.

Previously operating as Innovative Electric Services, we rebranded in 2025–2026 to better reflect our expanded focus on precision metering, revenue optimization, and practical training for Midwest utilities and electric cooperatives.

Watthour Solutions serves utilities and electric cooperatives in Missouri, Kansas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas.

Our work centers on helping utilities improve metering accuracy, identify billing errors, and recover lost revenue through detailed audits, field testing, and CT meter expertise. We believe in practical, field-tested solutions rather than theoretical approaches.
