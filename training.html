COMPREHENSIVE 8-MODULE CURRICULUM

What You’ll Learn

From foundational safety to advanced troubleshooting and modern metering challenges.

1
Safety for the Meterman

PPE requirements, lever bypass safety, arc-flash hazards, real-world scenarios and videos.

2
Introduction to the Electric Meter

Metering theory, history, modern trends, self-contained vs. instrument transformer metering, nameplates, form types, and wiring diagrams.

3
Introduction to Instrument Transformers

CTs and PTs, primary metering, calculating meter multipliers, nameplate information.

4
Demand and Time of Use Metering

Demand metering fundamentals, Time of Use (TOU), KYZ pulse metering.

5
Metering Math

Ratios, multipliers, cosine theory, vector analysis, transformer/CT/PT sizing, power factor, and troubleshooting with vectors.

6
Meter Site Testing Theory

In-service testing of instrument transformer sites, visual inspection, time load checks, vector analysis, and CT ratio testing.

7
Tips and Tricks

Real-world “war stories,” common issues discovered in the field, and practical solutions from experienced technicians.

8
Metering Roundtable Discussion

Open discussion on utility-specific challenges, solar generation, EV charging, sub-metering, and the future of metering.
